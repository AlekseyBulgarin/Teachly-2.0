name: teachly-b2b-copy
description: Simplify Teachly Showcase copy so non-technical educational business owners immediately understand the product and its value.
---

Use this skill for visible Showcase copy, CTAs and module descriptions.

Audience:
- owner of an educational business
- online school director
- product or business lead

Every module should quickly answer:
1. What is it?
2. What does it give the business?
3. What does it give students or teachers?
4. How does it connect with Teachly?
5. Is it Live, Coming Next or Planned?

Style:
- short headings
- concise paragraphs
- business value first
- professional Russian
- no hype
- no invented metrics

Avoid primary-page engineering jargon.

Translate technical concepts into simple business language.

Examples:
- tenant isolation -> Данные каждой организации изолированы
- learning evidence -> Teachly понимает прогресс и пробелы ученика
- AI traces -> Контроль и история работы AI
- knowledge governance -> AI работает с проверенными учебными материалами

Never invent:
- capabilities
- customer results
- ROI
- metrics

Preserve RU/EN i18n parity.
