# Product Open Questions

Questions are limited to decisions that materially affect scope, data ownership, authorization, or architecture.

## Product

- **IMPORTANT:** Which teacher workflow is the launch wedge: homework, training, assessments, or a balanced combination?
- **IMPORTANT:** What minimum teacher analytics makes the product valuable after the first assignment?

## Content

- **BLOCKING for launch content:** Which external sources are licensed for import, transformation, and student redistribution?
- **IMPORTANT:** Who owns validation and moderation of imported tasks and solutions?
- **IMPORTANT:** May teachers edit canonical content, or may they only compose activities from published versions?

## Task and Assessment

- **BLOCKING for evaluation scope:** Which answer types must be supported in the first content rollout?
- **IMPORTANT:** Is Informatics code execution required for MVP, or can it be deferred?
- **IMPORTANT:** Which assessment rules, timing, and scoring behaviors are required for the first slice experience?

## Learning

- **IMPORTANT:** What deterministic evidence and thresholds define mastery for a skill or topic?
- **LATER:** Should learning paths be teacher-authored first, system-generated first, or both?

## Users and Privacy

- **IMPORTANT:** What consent and approval process is required for guardian access to minors' data?
- **IMPORTANT:** Which progress, result, and homework fields may guardians see?
- **LATER:** Which CIS jurisdiction-specific retention, deletion, and minor-data obligations apply at launch?

## Monetization

- **LATER:** Which capabilities distinguish the free tier from paid independent-teacher usage?
- **LATER:** Which organization controls, analytics, and content rights require paid plans?

## B2B and Integrations

- **LATER:** Which external identity and SSO model is required for the first integration pilot?
- **LATER:** Which API resources and webhook events must become public integration contracts?

## AI

- **LATER:** Which student and teacher AI capabilities are worth their operational and review cost after the deterministic loop works?
- **LATER:** What categories of student data may be sent to external model providers?
