# Personas

## Classification

- **Confirmed:** roles and their broad responsibilities below.
- **Assumption:** access is granted through explicit relationships and memberships rather than a single user role field.
- **Proposed default:** permissions are scoped to a resource and organization context.

## Teacher

Goals:

- manage students and groups;
- prepare training, homework, and assessments;
- use imported and curated task content;
- see mistakes, progress, and weak topics;
- provide feedback and plan the next activity.

Needs a fast teacher workspace, reliable evaluation, explainable analytics, and safe AI assistance. A teacher may be independent or belong to multiple organizations.

## Student

Goals:

- follow assigned or self-directed learning;
- solve tasks and assessments;
- understand mistakes;
- see progress and recommended next activities.

Needs clear task instructions, deterministic results, useful feedback, and a low-friction solving flow. A student may exist independently and later join an organization.

## Parent or Guardian

Goals:

- monitor one or more children;
- review progress, completed work, results, and relevant activity.

Needs scoped, primarily read-only access and clear privacy boundaries. A guardian relationship is separate from organization membership and may require consent or approval rules.

## Organization

Goals:

- manage teachers, students, groups, programs, and organization-owned content;
- understand activity and outcomes across the organization.

Needs tenant isolation, delegated administration, auditability, and aggregated analytics. Organization capabilities can be introduced incrementally.

## Platform Administrator

Goals:

- manage platform safety and moderation;
- review content and abuse reports;
- maintain operational visibility.

Needs tightly controlled global access, audit logs, support tooling, and separation from normal tenant permissions.
